# Featured music experience — inside DANZSTAK Home Feed

## Scope
Keep the **public landing page unchanged**. Once a user enters the signed-in DANZSTAK app, present a compelling, tasteful featured music experience integrated with the mobile-first feed, without displacing Dailies, the four-post sequence or Rough Cuts.

## UX
- A compact featured-mix/music card or player integrated into the feed, with artist/title, cover art where licensed, obvious Play/Pause, and accessible controls.
- A refined, music-first atmosphere without autoplaying audible music unexpectedly or obscuring navigation.
- Do not overlap playback with feed videos, Dailies or Rough Cuts. Use the app-wide single active audio source.
- Preserve existing feed layout and core functions; preview on 360/390px phones first.

## Music
Owner cited **Return to Forever — Romantic Warrior (1976)** as a favorite and a reference for the breadth of musical culture. This is a jazz-fusion album, not a DJ mix. Do not invent an approved track or embed URL. Use an authorized externally hosted player only after source/selection approval; no rehosting of copyrighted audio or artwork.

## Acceptance
Public landing page remains pixel/functionally unchanged; signed-in feed has a working, tasteful featured-music experience. Test play/pause, media focus, navigation, mobile and desktop. PR and verified phone PWA preview; no unapproved production deployment.
