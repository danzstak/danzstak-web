# 03 — Chat experience

Design a familiar modern messaging interface with conversation list, active thread, message composer, unread indicators, timestamps, delivery/error states, and responsive mobile navigation. Preserve the existing DANZSTAK backend and authentication. Do not claim carrier phone/SMS functionality exists without a real provider and tested integration. User-owned and DANZSTAK-assigned numbers are separate planned product capabilities; do not silently implement billing or provision numbers.

**Acceptance:** send/receive persistence, reload, error recovery, mobile layout, keyboard navigation, and clear permission boundaries must be tested and reported.
