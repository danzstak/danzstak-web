# Task 11 — Checkout consent, partial use and chargeback evidence

## Owner direction (2026-11-16)
Maximum refund protection for DanzStak direct purchases (credits, contact unlocks, DanzStak numbers, AI EPK): the EU right of withdrawal, partial use, card-fee deduction, chargeback deterrence against "friendly" chargebacks, usage logs submitted to defeat chargebacks, and "the user must click an unchecked box or a dedicated confirmation". The final wording of ToS 8A waits for the lawyer (packet, Nov 20). Build the mechanics now, behind the current 8A text.

## Build
1. **Consent at checkout** (every DanzStak direct purchase). Use Stripe Checkout's native box: `consent_collection[terms_of_service]=required` (unticked by default, Stripe blocks payment until ticked; requires the Terms of Service URL set in Stripe → Settings → Public details) plus `custom_text[terms_of_service_acceptance][message]` with the owner's exact wording:
   "I agree to the Terms of Service and Refund Policy (Section 8A), request immediate access to my digital purchase, and acknowledge that I lose my statutory right of withdrawal once delivery or performance begins." (Link "Terms of Service" and "Section 8A" with markdown links.)
   Verify `session.consent.terms_of_service === "accepted"` in the `checkout.session.completed` webhook before granting anything. Stripe records acceptance but does **not** record our IP or timing for us, so also capture them ourselves (below). Same box on ticket and shop checkouts, with the marketplace wording (ToS 8C) instead of 8A.
   Before redirecting, our page also shows:
   - Shows the price, what counts as "used" for this item, and the refund rule.
   - Stored server-side: `CONSENT#<orderId>` with our server timestamp (UTC), the buyer's **full IP address** and user agent, terms version and hash, and the exact wording shown. Keep the full IP for the dispute window (180 days after the charge), then truncate it. Disclose this in the privacy policy.
   - After payment, also store **Stripe's own records**: the Checkout Session id, PaymentIntent and Charge ids, Stripe's `created` timestamps for the session and the charge, the `checkout.session.completed` event id and timestamp, and the Stripe Radar/charge `outcome` (risk level). Link them to the consent record, so the evidence shows our consent tick and Stripe's payment time side by side.
2. **Confirmation email** after payment, repeating the item, price, the consent given and the refund rule (the EU "durable medium"). Sent via SES us-east-1 like ticket emails.
3. **One-off charges only (owner, 2026-11-16): no credit bundles, no subscriptions.** Each purchase is one item tied to one use. OPEN QUESTION for the owner before building: today booking emails are bought 1–500 per checkout at $1.50 each (a bundle). Paying per single email means Stripe's fee (~2.9% + $0.30) eats ~23% of $1.50 and ~43% of a $0.75 contact unlock. Option A: one charge covers exactly the pitches the artist is sending now (e.g. 12 selected venues = one $18 charge, each consumed as it sends). Option B: raise single-item prices. Don't change pricing until the owner picks. Old text, kept until then: **Partial use**: credit packs track `creditsUsed` / `creditsUnused`. A refund request computes the amount from unused credits only. Until the lawyer answers Q1–Q2 in the packet, EU buyers within 14 days of purchase get unused credits refunded **without** fee deduction (feature flag `REFUND_EU_FULL_UNUSED=on`); everyone else gets current 8A (minus the Stripe fee, after accepting the exact amount).
4. **Usage logs** (append-only): every consumption event (email queued, email sent, contact shown, number activated, AI draft shown) with order id, timestamp and item id. The member can see their own log in Your data.
5. **Dispute evidence.** Stripe handles the dispute with the card network, but it doesn't win it for us: we must submit evidence before Stripe's deadline, and a dispute fee is charged either way. on Stripe webhook `charge.dispute.created`, assemble evidence automatically (consent record, terms version, confirmation email, usage log, account age, IP/device match) and submit via the Stripe Disputes API (`evidence` fields: `customer_purchase_ip`, `access_activity_log`, `refund_policy`, `refund_policy_disclosure`, `service_date`, `uncategorized_text`). Owner gets an email with the dispute and what was sent. Freeze the disputed items (revoke unused credits); don't auto-ban. Account suspension is a manual owner decision.
6. **Pre-dispute deflection**: a visible "Problem with a purchase? Ask us first" link on receipts and in Your data, answered within 2 business days.

## Tests
- Checkout without the box ticked → 400; the box is never pre-ticked.
- Consent record and confirmation email exist for every paid order.
- Partial refund equals unused credits × unit price (EU in-window: no fee deduction while the flag is on).
- A dispute webhook submits evidence once (idempotent) and freezes unused items.
- Usage log entries can't be edited or deleted by the app.
