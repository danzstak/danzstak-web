This Privacy Policy explains how DANZSTAK (“DANZSTAK,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, discloses, and protects personal information when you use danzstak.com, our social-networking features, and related services (the “Service”).

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide

  • Account details, such as your name, username, email address, password credentials managed by our authentication provider, and profile information.
  • Original content you create, including posts, comments, messages, photos, videos, event details, and other permitted uploads. Videos may contain the uploader's original music, but DANZSTAK does not accept standalone audio files or third-party copyrighted material.
  • Your communications with us, including support, moderation, privacy, and legal requests.
  • Transaction and booking information if paid features are introduced. Full payment-card data will be handled by authorized payment providers rather than stored by DANZSTAK.

Information collected automatically

  • Device, browser, IP address, language, approximate location derived from IP, and operating-system information.
  • Usage data, including pages viewed, features used, interactions, referrals, timestamps, and diagnostic logs.
  • Cookies and similar technologies used for secure sessions, preferences, fraud prevention, analytics, and—only where permitted—advertising.

Information from others

We may receive information from authentication providers, service providers, other users who interact with or mention you, and third-party integrations that you choose to connect.

Peer-to-peer calls

WebRTC calls require camera or microphone permission and establish an encrypted peer connection. DANZSTAK does not record call audio, video, or peer-to-peer chat in the current calling module. A call participant may learn technical connection information such as your IP address, and participants may independently record or capture communications using their device. Share call codes only with the intended participant.

2. How We Use Information

  • Provide, personalize, maintain, and improve the Service.
  • Authenticate users and protect accounts and sessions.
  • Publish and distribute content according to your settings and actions.
  • Enable social connections, messaging, music discovery, events, and bookings.
  • Detect spam, fraud, abuse, security incidents, and violations of our Terms.
  • Communicate service, safety, support, and—where allowed—marketing messages.
  • Comply with law, enforce agreements, and protect users, DANZSTAK, and the public.

3. Legal Bases for EEA and UK Users

Where applicable, we process information to perform our contract with you, comply with legal obligations, protect vital interests, pursue legitimate interests such as safety and service improvement, and based on consent where required. You may withdraw consent at any time without affecting earlier lawful processing.

4. How Information Is Shared

  • With other users and the public: profile and content information you choose to publish may be visible, copied, reshared, or indexed.
  • With service providers: hosting, authentication, storage, content delivery, security, analytics, customer support, email, and payment providers acting under appropriate terms.
  • For legal and safety reasons: when reasonably necessary to comply with law, respond to valid legal process, enforce terms, investigate abuse, or protect rights and safety.
  • Business transfers: in connection with a merger, financing, acquisition, reorganization, or sale of assets, subject to applicable law.
  • With your direction: when you connect an integration or otherwise ask us to share information.

We do not sell personal information for money. If future advertising practices constitute “sale” or “sharing” under applicable law, we will provide required notices and opt-out controls.

5. Cookies and Tracking

We use essential cookies for authentication, security, and core functionality. Non-essential analytics or advertising technologies will be used only with any consent required by law. Browser settings may block cookies, but essential features may then stop working. We will honor legally required preference signals, including Global Privacy Control where applicable.

6. Data Retention

We retain information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above, including providing the Service, maintaining security, resolving disputes, and meeting legal obligations. Retention depends on the information type, account status, sensitivity, legal requirements, and operational need. Deleted content may remain temporarily in backups or be retained when legally required.

7. Security

We use administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect information, including encrypted transport, access controls, secure authentication, monitoring, and restricted service access. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. International Transfers

Your information may be processed in countries other than where you live. Where required, we use recognized safeguards for international transfers, such as adequacy decisions, standard contractual clauses, or another lawful mechanism.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your information; restrict or object to processing; withdraw consent; opt out of certain sales, sharing, targeted advertising, or profiling; and appeal a denied request. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority. California residents may request information about collection and disclosure, request correction or deletion, opt out of sale or sharing, limit certain uses of sensitive personal information, and exercise rights without discriminatory treatment, subject to legal exceptions.

Submit requests to privacy@danzstak.com. We may verify your identity and authority before completing a request.

10. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 13, and users must be at least 13 or the higher minimum age required in their country. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children below the applicable minimum age. If you believe a child provided information unlawfully, contact us so we can investigate and delete it as required.

11. User Content and Public Information

Think carefully before posting. Public content may remain available through copies, screenshots, search engines, or other users even after you delete it from DANZSTAK. Do not post another person’s personal information without authority.
