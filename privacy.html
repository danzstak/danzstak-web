This Privacy Policy explains how DanzStak LLC (“DanzStak,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, discloses, and protects personal information when you use danzstak.com, our social-networking features, and related services (the “Service”).

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide

  • Account details, such as your name, username, email address, password credentials managed by our authentication provider, and profile information.
  • Original content you create, including posts, comments, messages, photos, videos, event details, and other permitted uploads. Videos may contain the uploader's original music, but DanzStak does not accept standalone audio files or third-party copyrighted material.
  • Your communications with us, including support, moderation, privacy, and legal requests.
  • Transaction and booking information if paid features are introduced. Full payment-card data will be handled by authorized payment providers rather than stored by DanzStak.

Information collected automatically

  • Device, browser, IP address, language, approximate location derived from IP, and operating-system information.
  • Usage data, including pages viewed, features used, interactions, referrals, timestamps, and diagnostic logs.
  • Cookies and similar technologies used for secure sessions, device preferences, and fraud prevention.

Information from others

We may receive information from authentication providers, service providers, other users who interact with or mention you, and third-party integrations that you choose to connect.

Messages and peer-to-peer calls

Direct text messages are stored so participants can retain their conversation history until they remove it from their account. WebRTC calls require camera or microphone permission and establish an encrypted peer connection between authorized friends or shared-group members. DanzStak temporarily processes call-signaling data but does not record call audio or video. A call participant may learn technical connection information such as your IP address and may independently record or capture a communication using their device.
