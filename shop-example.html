Shop basics

Photos

Square, natural light. For records: front, back and label. Show the real item, not a stock image.

Titles

Artist – Title (Format, Label Cat#). Collectors search for the exact pressing.

Condition

Discogs-style grades (M, NM, VG+, VG…) for the media and the sleeve, plus a line on anything a buyer should know.

Price and shipping

You keep your full price and your shipping. Buyers pay DanzStak's 6% fee (plus $1 on orders under $30), shown up front as one all-in price. Shipping can't cost more than the item.

Stock, drafts and where it shows

Set stock and mark sold out in one tap. Drafts stay private until you publish. Your shop lives on your profile, and published items also appear in the Bazaar.

Moving Day

Bring a whole Discogs, eBay or Shopify inventory, or a Discogs collection, in one go. It's free and runs from your own export.

AI shop (optional)

For $6.30, the AI shop drafts your shop and up to 10 listings from your photos. You review every word before anything is published.
